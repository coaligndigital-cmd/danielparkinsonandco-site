Common questions

What businesses usually ask.

What is Search & AI Visibility?

It is the work of making a business easier to find, understand and trust across traditional search, local results and AI-generated answers.

Does this replace SEO?

No. Strong technical SEO, useful content and credible authority remain the foundation. AI discovery adds another way those signals can be interpreted and surfaced.

Can visibility in AI answers be guaranteed?

No responsible adviser can guarantee inclusion. The work improves the durable signals that help search and AI systems understand the business.
