Common questions

What businesses usually ask.

What does Email Growth cover?

It covers the role of email in the customer journey, audience and proposition, campaign planning, automated journeys and useful performance measurement.

Do I need a large email list?

No. A smaller relevant list with a clear purpose can be more commercially useful than a large unengaged audience.

Can this improve existing campaigns?

Yes. Existing activity can be reviewed and improved before new journeys or campaigns are introduced.
