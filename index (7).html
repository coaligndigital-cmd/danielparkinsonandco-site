Common questions

What businesses usually ask.

What is a Marketing MOT?

A Marketing MOT is an independent review of your positioning, activity and customer journey. It identifies what is working, where value is being lost and what to prioritise next.

Who is it for?

It is designed for growing businesses whose marketing feels busy, unclear or disconnected from commercial priorities.

What do I receive?

You receive prioritised findings, practical recommendations and a clear 90-day action plan, followed by a walkthrough.
