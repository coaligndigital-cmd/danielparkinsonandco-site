Why this practice exists

Good thinking should stay close to the work.

I created Daniel Parkinson & Co. because too much marketing is separated into layers: one person sells, another plans and someone else delivers. Context disappears and clients spend time joining the pieces back together.

Here, strategy and delivery sit together. I learn the business, make the recommendations and remain involved in turning them into action.

My experience spans B2B, healthcare, regulated and specialist markets—places where clarity, evidence and commercial judgement matter.
