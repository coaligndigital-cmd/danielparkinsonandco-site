Who is responsible

Daniel Parkinson & Co. is responsible for information submitted through this website. Questions can be sent to hello@dpandco.co.uk or through the contact form.

Information collected

The enquiry form may collect your name, email address, organisation, area of interest and message. Basic technical and analytics data may also be collected where consent is given.

How it is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, provide requested services, maintain appropriate business records and improve this website. It is not sold.

Lawful basis and retention

Enquiry data is processed to respond to your request and, where relevant, to take steps before entering a contract. Records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose, legal obligations and legitimate business administration.

Your rights

UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to use of your data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Suppliers and international processing

Trusted providers used for hosting, forms and analytics may process limited data under appropriate safeguards. The contact form is provided by Web3Forms.
